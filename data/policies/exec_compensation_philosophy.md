# Executive Compensation & Org Planning Philosophy

*Classification: Restricted. Visible only to Admin-tier roles (CEO/CTO/
Executive). Not visible to department managers or employees.*

## 1. Compensation Bands
Compensation bands for leadership roles are benchmarked annually against
industry data and reviewed by the Executive Office and Board Compensation
Committee.

## 2. Org Design Principles
Department headcount planning follows a quarterly review cycle tied to
revenue-per-department and growth targets, owned jointly by Finance and the
Executive Office.

## 3. Succession Planning
Each department head must maintain a documented succession plan for their
role, reviewed annually by the Executive Office.

## 4. Confidentiality
This document and related compensation data must not be shared outside the
Executive Office and Board without CEO approval.
