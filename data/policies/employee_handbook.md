# FinPay Employee Handbook

## 1. Purpose
This handbook outlines the standards, benefits, and expectations for all FinPay
employees across departments.

## 2. Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Hybrid work is
permitted up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval.

## 3. Leave Policy
- 18 days of paid annual leave, accrued monthly.
- 10 paid sick days per year.
- 26 weeks of maternity leave; 4 weeks of paternity leave.
- Leave requests must be submitted via the HR portal at least 3 working days
  in advance, except for medical emergencies.

## 4. Code of Conduct Reference
All employees must comply with the FinPay Code of Conduct (POL-002). Violations
are reviewed by HR and, where relevant, Compliance.

## 5. Data Handling Expectations
Employees may only access data relevant to their department and role. Any
attempt to access another employee's personal data, or another department's
confidential data, without a documented business justification is a policy
violation and must be reported to Compliance immediately.

## 6. Expense Reimbursement
Standard reimbursement turnaround is 7 business days after Finance approval.
See the Finance Expense Policy (POL-003) for category-specific limits.

## 7. Grievance Redressal
Employees may raise grievances confidentially through the HR portal or by
emailing hr-grievance@finpay-demo.com. All grievances are reviewed within
5 working days.
