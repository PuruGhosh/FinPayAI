# FinPay Terms of Service (Summary)

## 1. Eligibility
You must be 18 years or older and complete KYC verification to use FinPay.

## 2. Account Usage
You agree to use FinPay only for lawful transactions. FinPay reserves the
right to suspend accounts suspected of fraudulent or prohibited activity.

## 3. Fees
Standard P2P transfers are free. Merchant payments may incur a processing
fee shown at the time of transaction. See the Offers page for current
promotions.

## 4. Liability
FinPay is not liable for losses due to unauthorized access resulting from
user negligence (e.g., sharing OTPs or passwords).

## 5. Dispute Resolution
Transaction disputes can be raised in-app under Help > Report an Issue.
FinPay aims to resolve disputes within 7 business days.

## 6. Changes to Terms
FinPay may update these terms with 30 days' notice via the app or registered
email.
