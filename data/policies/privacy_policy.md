# FinPay Privacy Policy (Summary)

## 1. Data We Collect
Name, contact details, government ID (for KYC), transaction history, and
device information.

## 2. How We Use Your Data
To process transactions, verify identity, prevent fraud, and comply with
regulatory reporting obligations.

## 3. Data Sharing
We do not sell personal data. We may share data with regulators, law
enforcement (where legally required), and payment partners strictly to
process your transactions.

## 4. Your Rights
You may request a copy of your data or request account deletion, subject to
regulatory record-retention requirements, by contacting
privacy@finpay-demo.com.

## 5. Data Security
We use encryption in transit and at rest, and role-based access controls to
limit internal access to personal data on a need-to-know basis.

## 6. Retention
Transaction records are retained for 5 years post-account-closure in line
with financial regulation.
