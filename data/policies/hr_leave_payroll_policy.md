# HR Payroll & Leave Administration Policy

*Audience: HR department employees only. This document covers administrative
process details, not employee-facing entitlements (see Employee Handbook,
POL-001, for that).*

## 1. Payroll Cycle
Salaries are disbursed on the last working day of each month. Off-cycle
payroll runs (e.g., final settlement) require HR Manager approval.

## 2. Salary Data Access
Salary figures are classified as PII. HR administrators may view salary data
for the purpose of payroll processing only. Any export of salary data outside
the payroll system requires HR Manager sign-off and must be logged.

## 3. Leave Balance Adjustments
Manual leave balance corrections require a written justification and
HR Manager approval, logged in the HR system audit trail.

## 4. Exit Process
On resignation or termination, HR must complete: final settlement calculation,
access revocation request to IT, and exit interview, within 5 working days of
last working day.

## 5. Background Verification
All new hires undergo background verification via an approved third-party
vendor before their start date is confirmed. Verification records are
retained for 3 years post-employment.
