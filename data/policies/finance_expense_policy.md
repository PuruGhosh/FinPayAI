# Finance Expense & Reimbursement Policy

*Audience: Finance department employees. General reimbursement summary for
all staff is covered in the Employee Handbook (POL-001).*

## 1. Approval Thresholds
- Below ₹5,000: Auto-approved by manager.
- ₹5,000–₹50,000: Requires Finance Lead approval.
- Above ₹50,000: Requires CFO/Finance Lead + Executive sign-off.

## 2. Monthly Ledger Close Process
Finance closes the transaction ledger on the 3rd business day of each month.
All settlement discrepancies must be flagged to the Finance Lead before close.

## 3. Vendor Payment Cycle
Vendor payments are processed in two batches: 1st and 16th of each month.
Emergency out-of-cycle payments require Finance Lead + Compliance sign-off.

## 4. Reconciliation with Compliance
Any transaction flagged by Compliance (status = 'flagged') is excluded from
standard ledger close until Compliance releases the hold.

## 5. Access to Transaction Data
Finance team members may query transaction records owned by department_owner
= 'FIN' for reconciliation purposes. Access to Compliance-flagged or
Customer Support-owned transaction records requires a cross-department
access request via the internal ticketing system.
