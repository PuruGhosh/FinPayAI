# Data Security & Access Control Policy

## 1. Principle of Least Privilege
Every employee and system account is granted the minimum data access required
for their role. Department-scoped access is the default; cross-department
access requires an approved access request.

## 2. PII Handling
Personally identifiable information (name, email, phone, address, national ID,
bank account, salary) must never be exported to unmanaged spreadsheets, personal
email, or third-party tools not on the approved vendor list.

## 3. System Access Reviews
Access permissions are reviewed quarterly by Engineering and HR jointly.
Dormant accounts (90+ days inactive) are automatically suspended.

## 4. Internal AI/Chatbot Tools
Any internal AI assistant with access to employee, customer, or transaction
data must enforce the same department-scoping and PII-masking rules as the
underlying systems it queries. AI tools must not be used to bypass access
controls that would otherwise apply to a human user in the same role.

## 5. Incident Reporting
Suspected data breaches or unauthorized access must be reported to
security@finpay-demo.com within 1 hour of discovery.
