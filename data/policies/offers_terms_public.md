# Offers & Rewards Terms and Conditions

## 1. General
All offers are subject to eligibility criteria and may be withdrawn or
modified at FinPay's discretion with prior notice where required.

## 2. Cashback Crediting
Cashback is credited to your FinPay wallet within 3–5 business days of a
qualifying transaction.

## 3. Referral Program
Referral bonuses are credited only after the referred user completes their
first successful transaction within 30 days of signup.

## 4. Fraud & Abuse
FinPay reserves the right to reverse cashback or referral bonuses obtained
through fraudulent activity, including self-referrals or transaction
manipulation.

## 5. Segment-Specific Offers
Some offers are targeted to specific customer segments (e.g., new users,
high-value users) and may not be visible or available to all customers.
